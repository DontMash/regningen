# Invoicing

This context describes the business issuing invoices and the people or organizations receiving them.

## Language

**Business**:
The organization or sole proprietorship issuing an invoice. Its name is distinct from the owner's name.
_Avoid_: Supplier, company (when the issuer might be a sole proprietor)

**Owner**:
The person who owns the business named on an invoice, not merely a contact person.
_Avoid_: Contact person, recipient

**Business Profile**:
The business's reusable identity, address, contact, tax, and payment details used on its invoices.
_Avoid_: Invoice details

**Invoice**:
A document from the business to a recipient listing goods or services and an amount due.
_Avoid_: Draft (when referring to an issued invoice)

**Invoice Draft**:
The business's editable working inputs for an Invoice, including incomplete or invalid values and an Invoice Position still being entered. It is distinct from the generated Invoice document.
_Avoid_: Invoice (when referring to editable working inputs)

**Invoice Language**:
The language of an Invoice's standard wording, distinct from the language used to operate the application. It does not change descriptions or other content supplied by the business.

**Invoice Position**:
A charge for a good or service on one invoice, with a description and gross amount. It is not a reusable catalogue entry.
_Avoid_: Product (when referring to a charge on an invoice)

**Recipient**:
The person or organization to whom an invoice is addressed.
_Avoid_: Business (when referring to the issuer)

**VAT ID (USt-IdNr.)**:
A business's VAT registration identifier, distinct from its tax number and an individual's personal tax ID.
_Avoid_: Personal Steuer-ID

**Tax Number (Steuernummer)**:
The number issued by a tax office to identify the business for tax purposes, distinct from its VAT ID.
_Avoid_: Personal Steuer-ID, VAT ID
